Refund Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

You can try the Free version first, forever. If the Full version isn't right for you, ask within 14 days of being charged and we'll refund you.

Monthly subscription

  • First payment: full refund if you ask within 14 days of being charged.
  • Renewals: full refund of a renewal if you ask within 7 days of it and haven't used Full version features since.
  • Cancelling: you can cancel anytime; the Full version keeps working until the end of the period you paid for. We don't refund partial periods otherwise.

Lifetime license

  • Full refund if you ask within 14 days of buying it. After a refund the key stops working.

If something's broken

If the Full version doesn't work on your setup and we can't fix it, we'll refund you regardless of the dates above.

How to ask

Reply to your Lemon Squeezy receipt email, or open an issue on GitHub with your order number (not your card or personal details). Refunds go back to your original payment method through Lemon Squeezy, usually within 5-10 business days. Your local consumer laws may give you more rights; this policy doesn't take those away.
