Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Short version: your stats stay on your PC. We never sell your data.

The app

  • Stays on your PC: your session stats, history, settings and log live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\HuntTracker. We never receive them.
  • Screen reading: the app reads the Mission Summary and lobby screens in memory to get your kills and Hunt Dollars. Screenshots are thrown away immediately; they're never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Update checks: the app asks GitHub for the latest release when it opens and every 30 minutes while it runs. GitHub sees that request (for example your IP address) under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • OBS Browser Source (off unless you turn it on): the app shows your stats card as a small web page on your own PC and home network, so OBS can add it, for example on a separate streaming PC. Only someone with your address (it contains a random key) can open it. It's served from your PC; nothing is sent to us.
  • No account, no Steam ID, no analytics, no ads.

Buying the Full version

  • Your purchase: Lemon Squeezy, our reseller, processes your payment. We receive your name, email address, country, order details and license key, but never your card details. See Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy.
  • License checks: when you enter your license key, and about every 12 hours after, the app sends the key and a random name for your PC to Lemon Squeezy to check your subscription is active. Your key is stored encrypted on your PC. No stats are sent.
  • How long we keep it: order and license records are kept while you're a customer, plus whatever tax law requires.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies or trackers of its own; GitHub may log visits as described in its privacy statement.

Your choices and rights

You can turn the Browser Source off, remove your license from a PC, cancel your subscription, or ask us to delete your data or tell you what we hold. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have further rights, such as access, correction and deletion; we'll honor those requests.

Children

Hunt: Showdown is rated for adults, and the Service isn't directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and note significant changes on this site.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub (don't post personal details publicly; we'll follow up privately).
